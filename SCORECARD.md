# Reflection Scorecard (Optional)

This is **not** a test someone passes or fails. It is a private reflection tool to help you notice patterns, trust your observations, and spot gaps you still need to explore.

> **Privacy tip:** Keep this offline or in a private note. Don't store identifying details about another person in public places, and never share it with them without their consent.

How to use: Copy this file, fill it in privately over several weeks, and revisit it. Scores should come from **observed behavior**, not hopes.

**Scale:** 1 = serious concern, 2 = some concern, 3 = unclear / not yet known, 4 = mostly good, 5 = strong

## Section 6: Reading your results

This is guidance, not a formula:

- **Mostly 4-5, and no safety concerns:** A promising foundation. Keep communicating, and keep observing over time.
- **Mix of 3s:** You may not know enough yet. Slow down, ask questions, spend time in different situations.
- **Several 1-2s in honesty, respect, consent, or safety:** Take these seriously. Talk to a trusted friend and consider stepping back.
- **High attraction but low scores elsewhere:** Common and worth noticing. Attraction is real but does not replace trust and safety.
- **Your own scores reveal patterns:** If you often feel anxious or "not enough", consider reflecting on it or talking to a counselor.
