# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a safe, welcoming, and supportive space for everyone who participates, regardless of sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, HIV or health status, body type, age, education, region, income, relationship structure, level of "outness," disability, ethnicity, nationality, or religion.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before reacting.
- Respect people's privacy, including not outing anyone or sharing their personal information.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it thoughtfully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the readers of this guide.
- Use inclusive, non-stigmatizing language, especially about health and sexuality.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or stigma, including against people living with HIV or other health conditions
- Doxxing, outing, or sharing private information, photos, or chats without consent
- Sexually explicit content or unwelcome sexual attention
- Promotion of scams, fraud, exploitation, or illegal activity
- Spamming, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Encouraging self-harm, violence, or abuse

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official channels) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

## Reporting

Report violations by opening a confidential issue (if available) or by contacting the maintainers through the contact method listed on the repository's profile. Reports will be handled with discretion and respect for the privacy of the reporter.
