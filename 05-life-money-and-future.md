# 05 - Life Circumstances, Money, and Future Plans

Practical compatibility is not unromantic. It's often what determines whether love can survive daily life.

*Suggested timing: developing stage; deeper financial and long-term topics before committing to exclusivity or cohabitation.*

---

## A. Work, study, and ambition

- [ ] What do they do, and do they find it meaningful or at least tolerable?
- [ ] Is their income stable? Do they have a plan for career growth?
- [ ] Are they working extreme hours (e.g., chronic overtime culture)? How does that leave room for a relationship?
- [ ] Is their workplace safe for being out? Do they feel they must hide?
- [ ] Are they a student or in civil service, state-owned enterprise, or other settings with specific pressures?
- [ ] How do they define success?

## B. Money habits and values

**Ask gently, share honestly**
- [ ] How do they handle money: saver, spender, balanced?
- [ ] Any significant debts (student loans, credit cards, online loans, family debt)?
- [ ] Do they send money to family regularly? How much, and is it expected to continue?
- [ ] Do they gamble, speculate heavily, or take risky financial bets?
- [ ] How do they feel about splitting expenses (AA), one person paying, or income-proportional sharing?
- [ ] Are they comfortable discussing money openly?
- [ ] Do they expect you to pay for gifts, trips, or expensive items?

**Red flags**
- Repeated requests for money, loans, or "emergency" help, especially early on
- Secrecy about debts that affect shared plans
- Pressure to invest in schemes, apps, or "opportunities"
- Using gifts or spending to control or guilt you

## C. Housing and location

- [ ] Which city do they live in, and do they plan to stay?
- [ ] Are they open to moving to a more LGBT-friendly or career-friendly city?
- [ ] Do they live with parents or roommates? How does that affect privacy and dating?
- [ ] Are they renting or do they own property? What is the long-term housing plan?
- [ ] Is the *hukou* (household registration) situation a factor in their city plans?
- [ ] Long-distance: how realistic is it? Who would move, and when?

## E. Time and attention

- [ ] How often do each of you want to see each other?
- [ ] How do you each feel about daily messaging?
- [ ] How do holidays work (Spring Festival, National Day)? Will one of you be required to go home and hide the relationship?
- [ ] How do you share time with friends and family?

## F. Future vision

Discuss these in a relaxed, non-pressured way.

1. Where do you see yourselves in 3, 5, and 10 years?
2. Is the relationship heading toward living together? Long-term partnership? Something else?
3. How do you each imagine aging, caring for parents, and building a life?
4. Do you imagine living in China long-term or going abroad (study, work, immigration)?
5. If one of you receives a major opportunity elsewhere, how would you decide together?

## G. Decision-making and teamwork

- [ ] How do you make big decisions: together, or one leads?
- [ ] How do you handle disagreements about money, location, or family?
- [ ] Do you both feel heard, and do both sets of needs count?

## H. Exclusivity and relationship structure

Make this explicit rather than assumed.

- [ ] Are you both looking for exclusivity, an open relationship, or something else?
- [ ] What counts as cheating or a boundary violation (flirting, apps, chat, sex, emotional closeness)?
- [ ] Are dating apps deleted or kept? What are the rules around them?
- [ ] How will you handle attraction to others?
- [ ] If rules change, how will you renegotiate?

> There is no single "right" structure, but both people must **know and agree** to the one you choose. Secretly breaking agreed rules is a breach of trust, not a different style.

## I. Practical milestones (before cohabiting or merging finances)

- [ ] Have we been together long enough to see each other under stress?
- [ ] Have we met each other's close friends?
- [ ] Have we discussed health, money, family, and exclusivity openly?
- [ ] Have we agreed on how to split costs and what happens if we break up?
- [ ] Do we have separate savings or exit options so neither of us is trapped?
