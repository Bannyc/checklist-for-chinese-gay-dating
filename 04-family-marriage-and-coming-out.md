# 04 - Family, Marriage Expectations, and Openness

For many gay men in mainland China, family expectations are the biggest practical factor in a relationship. This file helps you discuss them honestly **before** emotional investment becomes painful.

*Suggested timing: some topics early (relationship status, marriage plans), the rest in the developing stage and before committing.*

---

## A. The most important early question: marriage plans

Be direct and kind. Ask early, and answer honestly yourself.

- [ ] Are they currently single, not married, not engaged, and not in an arranged "blind date" process they are hiding from you?
- [ ] Do they plan to marry a woman at some point (to satisfy family or society)?
- [ ] Are they considering a **"cooperative marriage" (形婚)** with a lesbian woman or with someone else? If so, how would that affect your relationship, and are you comfortable with it?
- [ ] Do they believe they will eventually "have to" marry a woman? What would trigger it?
- [ ] If they feel family pressure, what is their plan, and is it realistic and ethical?

**Sample phrases**
- "I'd like to be upfront: I'm not looking for something that ends when family pressure peaks. How do you see that?"
- "Is marriage to a woman something you think you'll do?"

**Why this matters**
- Hidden marriage plans can end a relationship suddenly.
- Marrying a woman without her knowing her husband is gay causes serious harm to that woman. Ethical partners don't do this. (The community term *tongqi* 同妻 refers to wives of gay men in such situations.)
- Cooperative marriage arrangements raise ethical, legal, health, and emotional questions and can create complications for the existing relationship. If anyone considers it, it should be with full transparency from everyone involved.

## B. Coming-out status and openness

Know where each of you stands, and respect that "out" is a spectrum.

| Group | Do they know? | Do they accept? | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-----------------|-------|
| Parents | | | |
| Siblings | | | |
| Extended family | | | |
| Close friends | | | |
| Colleagues/boss | | | |
| Classmates | | | |
| Social media | | | |

**Ask**
- "Who knows about you? Who doesn't?"
- "How out do you want to be in the next few years?"
- "What would 'being a couple in public' look like for us: holding hands, photos, introducing each other to friends?"

**Check for compatibility**
- [ ] One partner wants to be fully visible; the other needs strict secrecy. Can you find a workable middle?
- [ ] Does their secrecy feel like **protection** (job, family safety) or **shame about you**?
- [ ] Can they introduce you at least to a few trusted friends, at a pace both of you can accept?
- [ ] Will they respect your privacy needs too?

> **Never out your partner or anyone else without their explicit consent**, including to friends, group chats, or on social media. Respecting others' pace is a basic form of care.

## C. Family dynamics

- [ ] Relationship with parents: close, tense, controlling, supportive?
- [ ] Do they have financial or caregiving obligations to parents or siblings?
- [ ] Are they an only child? Is there pressure for them to continue the family line?
- [ ] How do they handle pressure from parents (festival visits, matchmaking, "when will you marry")?
- [ ] Do they speak about their family with respect, even when disagreeing?
- [ ] Do they have a plan for caring for aging parents? How would that affect where you live?
- [ ] Do family members have a history of conflict, control, or violence?

## D. Children and family-building

- [ ] Does either of you want children? Biological, adopted, co-parenting, none?
- [ ] Are they aware that legal routes for same-sex couples to have and parent children in mainland China are limited and complex? Have they researched options (and the legal and ethical issues of each, including those abroad)?
- [ ] How important is "continuing the family line" to them and their family?
- [ ] If your answers differ, how do you each see it affecting the relationship?

## E. Long-term legal and practical protection

Same-sex marriage is not legally recognized in mainland China, so couples often plan with other tools. Discuss and consult a qualified lawyer.

- [ ] Are you both open to written agreements about shared property, finances, and living arrangements?
- [ ] Have you considered tools such as wills, adult guardianship agreements (意定监护), and clear documentation of shared assets? (Verify what is currently available and how they work.)
- [ ] Who would make medical decisions or be contacted if one of you were hospitalized?
- [ ] What are the housing arrangements (name on lease or deed, contributions, what happens in a breakup)?

## F. Social and community life

- [ ] Do they have LGBT or supportive friends?
- [ ] Do they feel isolated or connected to community?
- [ ] How do they view gay culture: embrace, ambivalence, or disdain?
- [ ] Can you both be yourselves in each other's friend circles?

## G. Cultural and religious beliefs

- [ ] Do religion or tradition affect their views of relationships, sex, or family?
- [ ] Do they have strong views on festivals, ancestor rituals, or obligations that could cause friction?
- [ ] How do they handle conflict between traditional expectations and their own life?

## H. Red flags in this area

- Vague answers about marriage plans, or "we'll see" that never gets clarified
- Hiding a girlfriend, fiancée, or wife
- Using family pressure to justify lying, rather than to explain a struggle honestly
- Treating you as a secret forever, with no path to any form of recognition
- Pressure on you to participate in a cooperative marriage or to hide the relationship completely without discussion
- Threatening to out you if you leave or disagree

## I. Healthy signs in this area

- They tell you the truth about their situation, even when it is hard.
- They are working toward a realistic plan that respects you.
- They protect your privacy as carefully as their own.
- They stand up for you in small ways when safe to do so.
- They are willing to have difficult but kind conversations with you about the future.
