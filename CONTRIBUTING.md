# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this guide! It works best when it reflects many real experiences.

## Who can contribute

Anyone. Especially welcome:

- People with lived experience dating as gay or queer men in mainland China
- Health professionals (infectious disease, sexual health, mental health)
- Lawyers or legal advocates familiar with Chinese law
- Counselors, researchers, and community organizers
- Translators who can help make the guide accessible in other languages

## Ways to contribute

1. **Open an issue** to suggest a new section, point out an error, or raise a concern.
2. **Submit a pull request** with edits or additions.
3. **Share updates** about services, laws, or resources that have changed.
4. **Review** others' pull requests with kindness and constructive feedback.

## Guidelines for content

- **Be respectful and inclusive.** No stereotypes, shaming, or discrimination, including toward people living with HIV, feminine men, people of any role or body type, or people who are not out.
- **Be accurate.** Where you state health or legal information, cite a credible source (WHO, national health authorities, peer-reviewed research, official legal texts) in the pull request description.
- **Be practical.** Prefer concrete questions, observations, and examples over abstract advice.
- **Stay mutual.** Items should be things the user would also be willing to answer or demonstrate.
- **Protect privacy.** Never include real names, identifiable stories, screenshots, or private chats. Anonymize and generalize any personal examples.
- **No promotion.** Don't add advertisements, affiliate links, or unvetted commercial services. Links to non-profit or public resources are fine, if verified.
- **No unsafe content.** Don't include instructions that encourage doxxing, outing, harassment, or illegal acts.
- **Keep the tone warm and clear.** Plain English is best, since many readers use English as a second language.

## Style

- Markdown, with clear headings.
- Use checklists (`- [ ]`) for things to observe or do, and short sample phrases in quotes for conversations.
- Keep sentences short.
- Mark timing suggestions as *early*, *developing*, or *before committing* where useful.

## Pull request process

1. Fork the repository and create a branch (e.g., `fix-health-links`).
2. Make your changes, and keep each pull request focused on one topic.
3. Describe what you changed and why, with sources where relevant.
4. A maintainer will review. Please be patient; we may ask for changes.

## Reporting problems

If you see content that is harmful, discriminatory, or unsafe, open an issue or contact the maintainers privately. We will review it promptly.

## Maintainers' commitments

- Keep the project non-commercial and community-focused.
- Review health and legal content carefully and update it when information changes.
- Respect contributor privacy.
- Apply the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) consistently.
