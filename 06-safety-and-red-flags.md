# 06 - Safety, Scams, and Red Flags

Most people you meet will be decent. But a few precautions protect you from the minority who are not, and in a context where outing carries real risk, safety includes **digital and privacy safety**.

*Suggested timing: use from the very first message.*

---

## A. Before meeting someone from an app

- [ ] Look for consistency between their photos, stories, and live conversation.
- [ ] Do a video call before meeting in person. Many scams and catfish disappear at this step.
- [ ] Do a reverse image search of their profile photos if something seems off.
- [ ] Don't share your full name, workplace, home address, ID number, or financial details early on.
- [ ] Check your own app privacy settings (distance display, photos, profile visibility, linked accounts).
- [ ] Consider using a separate phone number or account for dating apps.
- [ ] Be careful sharing intimate photos. Once sent, you cannot control them. If you do share, avoid showing your face or identifying details, and think about the worst-case scenario.

## B. First meetings

- [ ] Meet in a **public place** (cafe, restaurant, park, mall) during daytime for the first few meetings.
- [ ] Tell a trusted friend where you're going and with whom (a screenshot of their profile can help).
- [ ] Arrange your own transport and keep your phone charged.
- [ ] Don't leave your drink unattended.
- [ ] Limit alcohol on early meetings, so you keep clear judgment.
- [ ] Don't go to a private location (their place, a hotel) until you feel comfortable and have some trust built.
- [ ] Trust your instincts: if you feel uneasy, leave. You don't owe an explanation.
- [ ] Keep control of your belongings and documents.

## C. Common scams and risks

| Risk | What it looks like | How to protect yourself |
|------|--------------------|-------------------------|
| **Romance/investment scam** | Fast affection, then "an opportunity" to invest, trade, or gamble through an app | Never invest or transfer money at someone's suggestion. Stop contact. |
| **Money requests** | Emergencies, travel costs, medical bills, loans | Don't send money to people you haven't met and verified over a long time |
| **Extortion / blackmail** | Threats to expose your photos or orientation unless you pay | Do not pay. Save evidence, stop contact, and seek help from trusted friends, community groups, or authorities as appropriate. |
| **Robbery / "honey trap"** | Meeting at a private place where others appear | Meet in public; tell a friend; avoid isolated locations |
| **Fake profiles / catfishing** | Photos of models, refusal to video chat | Require a video call; reverse image search |
| **Paid-service scams** | Pressure to buy "membership", drinks at a bar, or tickets | Walk away from any date that turns into a spending setup |
| **Data theft** | Links, QR codes, or apps they ask you to install | Don't click unknown links or install unknown apps |

> If you are being blackmailed or threatened, you are the victim. The shame belongs to the person threatening you. Reach out to trusted friends, community organizations, or a lawyer.

## D. Relationship red flags (leave or seek help if present)

**Control and abuse**
- Threatens to out you, or uses your secrets against you
- Insults, humiliation, or contempt as regular behavior
- Isolates you from friends or community
- Demands passwords, location sharing, or constant reporting
- Extreme jealousy or accusations without basis
- Physical violence, intimidation, or damaging your belongings
- Forces or pressures sex, or ignores your "no"
- Sabotages protection (e.g., removing a condom without consent)

**Dishonesty**
- Hides a spouse, fiancée, girlfriend, or other partners
- Gives conflicting accounts of work, family, or history
- Lies about health or testing status
- Refuses to video chat, or to meet in public, indefinitely

**Pressure**
- Moves unusually fast, with intense declarations ("love bombing") followed by control
- Pressures you for money, favors, or secrecy
- Pushes for sex or unprotected sex as proof of love or trust

**Instability**
- Cycles between idealizing and devaluing you
- Refuses all accountability
- Frequent crises that always require your rescue
- Substance abuse that leads to harmful behavior

## E. Yellow flags (talk about it, observe closely)

- Slow replies, but willingness to explain
- Reluctance to introduce you to friends, with a plausible reason and a path forward
- Unresolved conflict with family, with honest effort to cope
- Past breakups with lots of blame; ask what they learned
- Limited experience in relationships, so differences in expectations need clear conversation

## F. Digital privacy and anti-outing practices

- [ ] Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication where possible.
- [ ] Be careful with photo metadata (location tags) and screenshots.
- [ ] Don't share partner photos or chats in group chats without consent.
- [ ] Keep dating app accounts separate from work or family social media.
- [ ] Review who can see your WeChat Moments and friend lists.
- [ ] Consider how mutual friends might see tagged or shared content.
- [ ] Agree with partners on rules for photos, tags, and posts.

## G. Digital and sexual consent

- [ ] Consent to photos or videos should be explicit and revocable.
- [ ] Never share or threaten to share intimate images of anyone. Doing so is harmful and may be illegal.
- [ ] If someone shares your images without consent, document the evidence and seek help.

## H. If you decide to leave

- You don't need a perfect reason; "this isn't working for me" is enough.
- Block and mute if you need to, especially if they are manipulative or threatening.
- Tell a trusted friend, especially if you feel unsafe.
- Change passwords and review privacy settings if you shared accounts or devices.
- Take care of yourself afterward: talk to friends, seek counseling if you need it.

## I. If you are in immediate danger

- Move to a safe, public place and contact trusted people.
- In China, the emergency police number is **110**; medical emergency is **120**. Use them when your safety or life is at risk.
