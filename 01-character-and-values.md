# 01 - Character and Values

Character is revealed by **patterns over time**, not by one good date. Use this file to know what to look for and what to ask.

*Suggested timing: start observing from day one; discuss deeper values in the developing stage.*

---

## A. Honesty and integrity

**Observe over time**
- [ ] Their stories are consistent (job, age, location, relationship history, family).
- [ ] They admit mistakes without excessive excuses or blaming others.
- [ ] They keep small promises (showing up on time, replying when they said they would).
- [ ] Their online profile matches reality (photos, age, job, relationship status).
- [ ] They don't lie "to be nice" about trivial things in ways that build up.

**Ask (gently)**
- "What does honesty in a relationship mean to you?"
- "Have you ever had to tell someone a hard truth? How did it go?"
- "What would you do if you realized you'd made a mistake that hurt me?"

**Concerning signs**
- Tells different versions of the same story
- Hides their phone, accounts, or whereabouts without explanation
- Gets angry when asked ordinary questions
- Claims to be single but shows signs of another partner, or a marriage plan

## B. Respect

**Observe**
- [ ] They treat waiters, drivers, delivery staff, and colleagues politely.
- [ ] They speak about exes and family without constant contempt.
- [ ] They respect your "no" the first time.
- [ ] They don't mock your appearance, job, accent, hometown, education, or hobbies.
- [ ] They are not contemptuous about gender, class, region (e.g., "country people"), or disability.
- [ ] They respect the privacy of others, including not outing people.

**Ask**
- "How did you handle the end of your last relationship?"
- "What did you learn from your exes?"

**Concerning signs**
- Jokes that are "just teasing" but consistently put you down
- Pressure after you decline (sex, photos, meeting, money)
- Contempt toward feminine gay men, "bottoms", "tops", or any group (internalized stigma turned outward)

## C. Responsibility and reliability

- [ ] They hold a steady job or study routine, or have a plan if between jobs.
- [ ] They manage their time and obligations reasonably.
- [ ] They take care of their health and living space at a basic level.
- [ ] They take responsibility for their choices instead of blaming parents, society, or "fate".

**Ask**
- "What are you working toward right now?"
- "What was the hardest thing you've had to take responsibility for?"

## D. Kindness and empathy

- [ ] They notice and respond when you're upset or tired.
- [ ] They can apologize sincerely and change behavior.
- [ ] They show care for friends, family, or animals without it being performative.
- [ ] They can celebrate your good news without competing.

## E. Ethics around relationships and sex

- [ ] They accept a clear definition of exclusivity once agreed.
- [ ] They don't use sex or affection as a reward, punishment, or bargaining chip.
- [ ] They understand and respect consent, including in established relationships.
- [ ] They don't pressure you into unsafe practices.
- [ ] They disclose honestly if they are seeing others before exclusivity is agreed.

## F. Attitude to power and control

- [ ] They are comfortable with you having your own friends and interests.
- [ ] They don't require passwords, location sharing, or constant reporting.
- [ ] Disagreements don't turn into threats (e.g., "I'll tell your family", "I'll expose you").
- [ ] They don't use your coming-out status as leverage.

> **Critical:** In a context where being outed can be costly, any threat to out you is a serious form of abuse. Treat it as a red flag that justifies leaving.
