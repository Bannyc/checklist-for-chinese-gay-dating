# 00 - Self-Reflection: Know Yourself First

Before evaluating anyone else, get clear about what *you* want and can offer. Most relationship mismatches begin with unexamined expectations.

*Suggested timing: before you start dating, and revisit every few months.*

---

## 1. Why do I want a relationship now?

- [ ] Am I looking for companionship, love, sex, stability, a family-like partnership, or an escape from loneliness or family pressure?
- [ ] Would I still want this person if I were not lonely?
- [ ] Am I using a relationship to avoid dealing with something else (family conflict, self-acceptance, career stress)?

## 2. My non-negotiables (deal-breakers)

Write down 3-7 things you genuinely cannot accept. Examples to consider:

- [ ] Dishonesty about relationship status or marriage plans
- [ ] Violence, threats, or controlling behavior
- [ ] Unwillingness to practice safer sex or get tested
- [ ] Planning a "cooperative marriage" or a marriage to a woman while dating me
- [ ] Heavy substance use that affects the relationship
- [ ] Disrespect toward my privacy or my boundaries
- [ ] Fundamental differences on children, location, or exclusivity

> Tip: A deal-breaker is something you would leave over. A preference is something you'd like. Don't mix them up.

## 3. My flexible preferences

Things you'd like but could compromise on (height, hobbies, appearance, education, religion, etc.). Be honest about whether these are real needs or social conditioning.

## 4. My own baggage and growth areas

- [ ] How do I behave when I'm jealous, anxious, or angry?
- [ ] Do I have internalized shame about being gay, and how does it show up?
- [ ] Do I tend to idealize people early and then feel disappointed?
- [ ] Do I avoid conflict, or escalate it?
- [ ] Do I communicate needs directly or expect people to guess?
- [ ] Have I processed past relationships and breakups?

## 5. My own situation (what I'd need to disclose)

Be ready to answer the same questions you ask:

- [ ] My coming-out status and who knows
- [ ] My family expectations and pressure
- [ ] My health status and testing habits
- [ ] My finances, debts, and obligations to family
- [ ] My plans for the next 3-5 years
- [ ] My past relationships and why they ended

## 6. My boundaries

- [ ] Privacy: photos, real name, workplace, social media visibility
- [ ] Sex: pace, practices, protection, frequency, what I will not do
- [ ] Money: how I want to handle shared costs
- [ ] Time: how much independence and alone time I need
- [ ] Exclusivity: what it means to me, and whether I'm open to alternatives

## 7. Support network

- [ ] Do I have friends or community I can talk to, so that this one person is not my only support?
- [ ] Do I know where to get a confidential health check and mental health support in my city?

---

**Reflection prompt:** If a friend described the person I'm dating exactly as I would describe them, what would I tell my friend?
